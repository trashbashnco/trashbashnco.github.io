Let go of everything. First.

Two players. Six characters each. A pile of Resolve chips. About twenty minutes.

§1 Characters

Every card is something you bought.

Each character has a unique ability, and a cost printed at the top left. When you have enough Resolve, you can choose to let go of that character: pay its cost and remove it from the game.

The cost at the top left is the initial cost. The cost after rotation is the current cost. You always pay the current cost.

§2 Resolve

The one resource.

Resolve is what it takes to let go. You gain it every turn, some characters give you more, and you spend it to let characters go. Track it with chips: take one when you gain Resolve, hand them back when you spend it.

it is willpower, basically. the longer you hold on, the more it costs.

§3 Abilities

Three triggers.

Every character has one ability. The tag in front of it tells you when it fires.

OnceActivate it one time, any time.Use it immediately or save it. Once used, it is spent for the rest of the game.
Every turnRepeats on all your turns.As long as the character is on your field, it fires every turn, for the whole game.
Let goFires only when you let it go.Example: [LET GO] Let go of an additional character whose current cost is below 3.

There is no limit to how many abilities you can activate in a turn.

§4 Building a deck

Six characters. Twenty-four Resolve.

Choose 6 character cards to bring into the game. Add up their initial costs. The total may be at most 24.

Cheap cards are easy to let go of and do little. Expensive cards are powerful and hard to part with. Twenty-four forces you to carry some of each.

§5 The table

Where everything goes.

Shuffle your deck. Draw one card face-up onto your field. Start with no chips. The first player skips their first draw.

§6 Your turn

Five steps, every turn.

  1. 1
    Draw.Take the top card of your deck and put it on your field, face up. (Skip if your deck is empty.)
  2. 2
    Gain Resolve.Add 1, plus whatever your Every turn cards give you.
  3. 3
    Use abilities.Fire any Once cards you want. No limit.
  4. 4
    Let go of one card.Pay its current cost in Resolve. Remove it from the game. If it has a Let go ability, it fires now.
  5. 5
    Rotate.Turn every character still on your field 90° clockwise. That ends your turn.
§7 Rotating

The longer you keep it, the more it costs.

At the end of every turn, each character still on your field gets harder to let go of. Show this by rotating the card 90° clockwise. Each rotation raises its cost.

A character can be rotated at most three times. After the third, it stays at that cost for the rest of the game. How much the cost rises is printed on the card; most go up by one per rotation, a few go up by more.

Initial costWhat is printed at the top left. Current costIts cost at the rotation it is on now. This is what you pay.
How to win

Own nothing.

Let go of every character on your field and empty your deck. The first player to do both wins.
